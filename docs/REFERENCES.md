# References

- [RAD Expansion Unit](https://github.com/frntc/RAD) — original hardware and firmware.
- [RAD-Doom](https://github.com/frntc/RAD-Doom) — bus, video and SID foundation;
  [v01 SD release](https://github.com/frntc/RAD-Doom/releases/tag/v01).
- [Wolf4SDL](https://github.com/KS-Presto/Wolf4SDL) — pinned engine and its README/license choices.
- [Circle](https://github.com/rsta2/circle), [circle-stdlib](https://github.com/smuehlst/circle-stdlib)
  and [circle-newlib](https://github.com/smuehlst/circle-newlib) — runtime source.
- [DOSBox DBOPL as retained by Wolf4SDL](https://github.com/KS-Presto/Wolf4SDL/tree/dc8b250af35fb0ace68db5eb879490b50068c20e/dosbox)
  — synthesis implementation, not the alternative MAME emulator.
- [Pex Mahoney's technical paper](https://livet.se/mahoney/c64-files/Musik_RunStop_Technical_Details_by_Pex_Mahoney_Tufvesson_v2.pdf)
  — original sample playback technique; see RAD-Doom's source attribution.
- [c64cast measured UltiSID table](https://github.com/kfox/c64cast/blob/34d350de020a3fc1ccfba61396402b12f9beb3e8/scripts/diags/mahoney_measured_tables.json)
  — imported MIT-licensed data, measurement conditions and source revision.
- [Ultimate sample playback report](https://github.com/GideonZ/1541ultimate/issues/650)
  — related UltiSID limitations, not a diagnosis of every setup.
- [Wolfenstein 3D on GOG](https://www.gog.com/en/game/wolfenstein_3d)
  — legal purchase source identified by the maintainer; no game data is mirrored.
- [Raspberry Pi boot firmware](https://github.com/raspberrypi/firmware)
  — separate boot files with their original licenses.
- [GNU GPL distribution FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html#UnchangedJustBinary)
  — source obligations relevant to future binary releases.

Exact code revisions are in UPSTREAM.lock.json. Store observations are dated
in GAME_DATA.md; links are references, not endorsements or a promise of availability.
